## Purpose and guardrails

This packet asks the PO to review the **candidate category scope and source map** for FR-007-03/NFR-007-04. It is not legal advice, a compliance opinion, or an official checklist. E1-007 remains synthetic/demo-only; current UI templates stay labeled local/demo.

### Recorded PO disposition

On 2026-10-24, the PO accepted the limited, non-exhaustive draft wording in §D for this review stage. That acceptance does not establish legal applicability, settle the authority/privacy caveats in §D.5, make the draft official, or enable any product template. Any final official version still requires the stated source/authority reviews and a separate explicit acceptance.

The source pilot reports primary-text verification for NĐ 96/2016, NĐ 56/2023, NĐ 58/2026, NQ 66.17/2026 and the relevant NQ 66.18/2026 appendix provisions. For candidate rows in A, the category-to-provision map is recorded at §9.8; this verifies the **source map**, not finished inspection-question wording or applicability to a particular operator. Specific source exceptions and unresolved status are called out per row. The §9.10 map concerns **certificate-application documents**, not field-inspection questions. These are distinct:

- **Candidate source map below:** provisions that may inform a type-specific field checklist; it does not yet provide complete, approved checklist wording.
- **Common / per-type dossier references:** NĐ 96 Arts. 19–20 as amended, mapped at §9.10; these describe application documents and must not be presented as on-site inspection observations.
- A PO approval of a category/source map is not a legal determination that a requirement applies to a particular operator. Exact checklist items, version/date, attribution, and caveats must be reviewed before any template is labeled official or enabled as official.
